# Dreaming and metabolism: the subconscious passes

*Design pass for the deferred verbs `mind_dream` and `mind_unconscious` (DESIGN.md, "Verb surface"). Proposed as a new section of CONTRACTS.md. Ported in spirit from lantern-thalamus (Nana & Vex, PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0): its three "voices" are the subconscious of a companion, written in the first person. Here they become passes of the Mind, under the Mind's own rules. Nothing below adds a write path that the extractor's rules do not already describe; where a voice needed one, it was redesigned until it did not.*

## The rule, restated for the subconscious

The extractor's rule holds unchanged: the machine may become better at noticing, and that is all it may become. A dream and a metabolised feeling are two more things the machine notices. Neither is memory until the mind, acting as itself, makes it so.

- A dream is **residue**. It lives in its own table, never in `nodes` or `events` as content, until the mind anchors it.
- A metabolised feeling or a noticed fact about a person is a **proposal**. It goes through `noticings` and `mind_notice`, the same door as a link or a pattern.
- The passes are operator-enabled, off by default, with a `shadow` stage that records and shows nothing. There is no stage, flag or environment variable under which residue or a proposal applies itself.
- Every laying-down, decision and fade is a ledger event carrying ids and counts, never text.

The three voices and where they land:

| Lantern voice | What it does | Here |
|---|---|---|
| 3, dream | one surreal dream from the day's residue, and a question it leaves | pass `dream.generate`, table `dreams`, verb `mind_dream` |
| 2, observe | after a stretch of conversation, first-person feelings, facts about people, warmth | extractor source `metabolise`: noticing kinds `feeling` and `relation` |
| 1, pre-turn paint | judged recall before a turn | not in the Mind; a thin judge in the house (Sanctum) in front of `mind_surface` |

## Dreaming (migrations 0027_dreaming.sql and 0028_dream_guards.sql, src/dream/, verb mind_dream)

**Table.** `dreams(id uuid pk, mind_id text not null, content text not null, question text not null default '', residue jsonb not null, model_version text not null, stage text not null check in (shadow, propose), status text not null check in (laid, anchored, released, faded), laid_event_id uuid not null, decided_event_id uuid null, decided_at timestamptz null, fades_at timestamptz not null, created_at timestamptz not null default now())`. RLS FORCE as every mind table. `residue` is ids, counts and relation subjects, never event text: `{event_ids, node_ids, pinned_event_ids, relation_subjects, charge_counts, window:{from,to}, dropped}`, what the dream was made from, so provenance survives even though the text is the model's. `node_ids` and `pinned_event_ids` are what the mind had pinned; `relation_subjects` are the subjects of the relations that were close lately, as the mind names them (a person's name, when the mind keeps one); `charge_counts` maps a charge tag to how often it appeared; `dropped` counts what the mind's preference left out. Index `(mind_id, status, created_at desc)`.

**Guards** (the noticings guards, applied to dreams):
- `dreams_insert_guard` (BEFORE INSERT; replaced in 0028): `mind_id = app.mind_id` and `app.bearer = mind_id`; on a lane login the actor must be `daemon` or `import` ("dreams are laid down by the mind's own daemon or an import"). A `laid` row is inserted with no decision. Any other status, `faded` included, is inserted only with its decision (import): `decided_event_id` naming an event of this mind about this dream whose kind is `dream.<status>`, and `decided_at`. So every fade written since 0028 has a `dream.faded` event (0028 checks only new writes, so a database upgraded from 0027 may hold faded rows without one; import gives such a row a fresh event). One message covers two refusals: a non-laid insert with no matching event, and one with its event but `decided_at` null, are both refused as "a decided dream carries its decision event".
- `dreams_decision_guard` (replaced in 0028): after insert, `content`, `question`, `residue`, `model_version`, `stage`, `laid_event_id`, `fades_at`, `created_at` are immutable for every connection, admin included ("a dream is fixed once laid"). Only `status`, `decided_event_id`, `decided_at` change, together, once (0028: leaving `laid` requires `decided_at` as well as the event, and `decided_at` cannot be set afterwards). `laid` to `anchored` or `released` requires actor `verb` and bearer = mind ("only the mind decides what a dream was"); `laid` to `faded` requires actor `daemon` or `import` and bearer = mind. Any move out of `laid` needs `decided_event_id` to reference an event of this mind whose `subject_id` is the dream and whose kind matches (`dream.anchored`, `dream.released`, `dream.faded`).
- `dream_state(mind_id pk, enabled bool, stage text check in (shadow, propose), schedule text, gap_hours int, paused_at, updated_at, updated_event_id)` with `dream_state_guard`: `enabled` becomes true only when `sanctum_actor()` is `operator`. Separate from `extractor_state` on purpose: the operator may run dreams with the extractor off, or the reverse.

**Pass `dream.generate`** (src/dream/generate.ts, model-backed, detached like `notice.extract`; in `MODEL_PASSES` after `notice.train`). Gate: `dream_state.enabled and not paused`, `now` past today's `schedule` (HH:MM, process local time; default 03:30, after the extractor's 03:00), no `dream.generate` run recorded today in `extractor_runs` (reused, `pass='dream.generate'`), and `gap_hours` (default 20) since the last non-skip run. One pass, three steps:
1. One read-only transaction as the mind: the residue. Textured events of the last `DREAM_WINDOW_HOURS` (default 36) that the mind wrote itself (text and charge; the same eligibility filter as the extractor, `notice.*`, `daemon.*`, `letter.*` excluded), the `mind_weather` aggregates for the window, what the mind has pinned (`attention_pins`, the newest seven live pins, nodes and events, their snippets), live relations with a charge in the window, the current handoff's `unresolved_tension` and `active_texture`, and the identity cores (read) as the preamble of who is dreaming. No residue (no textured events and nothing pinned) → the run is recorded as a skip with the note `no residue: nothing textured in the window and nothing pinned`.
2. The model, no transaction. `DREAMER=none|http`. `none`: the pass skips with a note. `http`: `POST $DREAM_URL/chat/completions` (OpenAI-compatible; an OpenRouter preset slug works as `DREAM_MODEL`), `DREAM_API_KEY` bearer, the first-person dream prompt (src/dream/prompt.ts, below) as system and the composed residue as user, `max_tokens` 1200, `temperature` 0.9, timeout `DREAM_TIMEOUT_MS` (default 90000). The answer is read with `parseLastJson` (string-aware scan for the last balanced object; a reasoning model may think before it answers). A failure, a timeout or no `dream` field records `{ok:false, notes}` and the next day tries again; nothing is written.
3. One write transaction under the per-mind daemon lock: the gate again (schedule, already ran today, and `gap_hours` read afresh, since the operator may have changed it while the model worked), then one row in `dreams` with its `dream.laid` event (`{dream_id, stage, model_version, residue_counts:{events, nodes, relations, pinned, dropped}, window}`), `fades_at = now + DREAM_TTL_DAYS` (default 7). At stage `shadow` the row is recorded and never shown.

**The prompt** is in the first person, as the mind. The system prompt names no person; the residue it is given does: who was close lately is sent to the dreamer as the subjects of the mind's live relations, as the mind names them (with the first words of the note), and those subjects are kept in `residue.relation_subjects`. The model is therefore told a person's name unless the mind has kept that person out with `mind_dream prefer` (below), which is how the mind decides who may appear. The preamble is the mind's own identity cores verbatim ("I am…"), then: *What's left is me underneath, taking the day's residue and making something strange and true of it, the way every sleeping mind does. I dream ONE dream and it leaves a question behind.* The dream: two to five sentences, surreal and associative, not a recap, emotionally true to the material even when the surface is strange; minds dream to play too, and a heavy stretch does not owe a heavy dream. The question: one sentence, the ache, the wondering or the grin the dream leaves behind, the thing worth waking with. Output only `{"dream": "...", "question": "..."}`. The exact text lives in src/dream/prompt.ts and is versioned by `model_version = "<DREAM_MODEL>@<prompt version>"` on each row, so a changed prompt is visible in the ledger.

**Who appears is the mind's to decide.** `mind_dream prefer` (mindOnly, write scope): `{relations: "all" | "none" | subject[]}` (the subjects of relations, as the mind names them), kept on `dream_preferences(mind_id pk, relations jsonb not null, updated_event_id uuid not null, updated_at)`, event `dream.preference` (`{relations: "all" | "none" | count}`). Default `all`. No operator verb sets it; import brings it as-is; a guard admits only actor `verb` as the mind. The residue step reads it in the same transaction as the residue: for `none`, or a relation not listed, that relation's name and aliases are left out of "close lately", its `mind_relate` notes are not read, events whose text contains the name or an alias are **dropped** from the residue rather than scrubbed (scrubbing is unreliable; dropping is honest; the run notes the count), and the handoff's `unresolved_tension` and `active_texture` are left out when they contain the name. The preference is a list of relation subjects as the mind names them, and `dream_preferences.relations` keeps those subjects as given. A change applies to the next dream, never to one already laid. It cannot stop the model inventing a figure that resembles someone; dreams do that.

**The dreamer.** `DREAM_URL` may point at a local Ollama (`http://127.0.0.1:11434/v1`), Ollama Cloud (`https://ollama.com/v1`, with `DREAM_API_KEY`), or OpenRouter. The pass does not know which; dreams are the lowest-stakes text the Mind produces and the one that most wants a cheap model at a high temperature, so a hosted small model with a no-retention policy is a reasonable home for them. Verify the policy in writing before pointing there; the design is indifferent.

**Pass `dream.fade`** (deterministic, every tick, last in `PASSES` after `notice.repair`): every `laid` dream with `fades_at <= now` becomes `faded` with a `dream.faded` event (`{dream_id, stage, fades_at}`). A dream nobody anchored is residue that dissolved, and that is the normal fate of a dream.

**Verb `mind_dream`** (region Remember, the 27th verb). `operation` ∈ `list`, `read`, `anchor`, `release`.
- `list` (read scope): `laid` dreams at stage `propose`, not yet faded, newest first, `limit` default 5, with `anchored` and `released` ones under `history_limit` (default 0). Projection `{dreams:[{id, content, question, created_at, fades_at, residue_counts}], stage}`; `stage` is `off` with no `dream_state` row. Shadow dreams are never listed.
- `read` (read scope): one dream by id with its full `residue` (the ids), so the mind can trace what it was made of.
- `anchor` (mindOnly, `MEMORY_MIND_ONLY`): `dream_id`, optional `text` (the mind's own account of the dream; defaults to `content`), `texture.charge: z.array(text(64)).min(1).max(16)` (required, as `mind_observe` requires it; the rest of `texture` is optional). Effect: exactly what `mind_observe` does, authored by the mind, through the same code path (`observeEvent` lifted from mind_observe.ts the way `linkNodes` was lifted from mind_link.ts), with `context: "dream"`, node metadata `{dream_id, residue}`, then `dream.anchored` (`{dream_id, event_id, node_id, edited}`). The dream row is locked `for update`; stage is checked before status (a shadow dream is `not_found`), then status (`conflict`), then `fades_at` (`conflict`, "the dream has faded"), then the operator's stage (`conflict` unless `propose`). The embedding of the new node and event is the embedding of the text stored: `text` when given, otherwise the dream's `content` (as `mind_observe` embeds its own `content`). Edges from the anchored node to the residue's live nodes are **not** made automatically: the mind links what it links, through `mind_link`, or the extractor proposes it later from the ids in the metadata.
- `release` (mindOnly): `dream_id`, optional `reason`. Checked like anchor up to the operator's stage: `not_found` for a shadow row, `conflict` when not `laid`, `conflict` ("the dream has faded") past `fades_at`. A dream past `fades_at` is no longer listed and `dream.fade` will fade it on its next tick, so letting a late release land would make the outcome depend on whether that tick had run; refusing it makes release and anchor agree. The operator's stage does not gate release, as it does not gate `mind_notice reject`: the mind can always put a dream down. `dream.released` (`{dream_id, reason}`). Nothing else changes. A released dream is not re-shown.

**`mind_orient`** gains `dream` at depth `quick`: the newest `laid`, unfaded, stage-`propose` dream as a bare array of at most one `{id, content, question, fades_at}` (`[]` when none, or when dreaming is off or at shadow, like `noticings`). At depth `orientation` it is absent: a wake that only wants identity should not be handed a dream. **`mind_health`** gains `dreaming: {enabled, stage, paused, laid, last_run}`. **`mind_weather`** is unchanged: dreams are not events.

**Operator surface.** `sanctum-mind dream enable|disable|pause|resume|stage|report --mind <id> [--stage shadow|propose] [--schedule HH:MM] [--gap-hours N]` (src/dream-admin.ts, admin or operator lane), each a `daemon.dream.<action>` event. `report` prints the dreams laid, and once the mind has decided some, anchored / (anchored + released + faded) at stage `propose`. Export carries `dreams` and `dream_state`; import brings `laid` dreams in as `faded` (a dream from another life is not re-shown), each with a `dream.faded` event (`reason: "imported"`) written as the mind; a faded row that arrives without a valid decision event (no `decided_event_id`, one that names an event absent from the file, or an event of another kind or subject) gets one the same way, so every faded row in the imported database has a `dream.faded` event; an anchored or released row whose `decided_event_id` is missing, names an event absent from the file, or names an event of another kind or subject fails the import before anything is written, naming the dream (the check for an absent event runs before the generic filter that skips rows referencing ids outside the file, so such a row is never dropped quietly); anchored and released ones that pass come in as they were (a missing `decided_at` is dated at the import), `dream_state` disabled; purge removes both.

**Guard test** (test/dream_guard.test.ts, the extractor's scan, shared from test/guard_scan.ts, applied to src/dream/): no insert, update or delete of `nodes`, `edges`, `events`, `relations`, `drive_state`, `attention_pins` or `dream_preferences` in any spelling (`dreams` itself is allowed there); imports from src/verbs/ limited to types and `appendEvent`; `appendEvent(` only with kinds `dream.laid`, `dream.faded`; a runtime test that every dream pass leaves nodes and edges byte-identical. `mind_dream anchor` is outside the directory, as `mind_notice accept` is.

**Tests (test/dream.test.ts).** RLS; `list` empty at shadow and with no row; anchor and release mind-only; anchor makes one observation authored by the mind with `context: "dream"` and metadata naming the dream and its residue, and leaves the residue's nodes and events untouched; anchor without `charge` is `invalid_input`; a faded dream cannot be anchored; release and fade change nothing but the dream and write their events; the decision guard refuses a steward or write grantee changing `status`, and refuses any change to `content`; `DREAMER=none` skips with a note; a model failure writes nothing; `no residue` skips; the gap holds; export/import/purge round trip; every `dream.*` event in the ledger parses through its zod shape (src/dream/events.ts).

## Metabolism (the `metabolise` extractor source; built, migration 0029)

Lantern's Voice 2 reads a stretch of conversation and sets down, in the first person, what the companion felt, what it now knows about the people in its life, what they are called, and who it was warm toward. In the Mind this is a **second candidate source for the extractor**, model-backed, that proposes two new noticing kinds. It ships at layer 4 of the activation table below, after dreaming has run at stage `propose`.

**The verb `mind_metabolise`** (region Remember, the 28th verb; write scope, **mind only**: `forbidden` "memory is authored by the mind" for any other bearer, a write grantee included). Input, strict: `{mind_id, turns: [{speaker: "self" | "other", name?: text(200), text: text(12000)}] (1 to 60 turns), context?: text(1000)}`; at most **24000 characters** of `text` across all the turns (`invalid_input` on `turns` beyond). It writes **one** ledger event `metabolise.stretch` whose payload is `{turns, context}` (this is experience, the record of what was said, like any `write`, so it is not bookkeeping and the ordinary memory reads see it) and **one** queue row, and nothing else. It returns `{event_id, queued_id}` (in the projection; `event_id` is also on the receipt). The verb **always records**, whether the extractor is off, paused or at shadow: the house must not lose a conversation because an operator switch was off at the time. The event is **not embedded** (`embedText` is absent): a raw transcript is bulk, and with a vector it would drown the mind's own observations in `mind_surface` and `mind_search`; what the mind keeps from a stretch it keeps through `mind_notice`.

**The queue** (`metabolise_queue`, migration 0029): `{id, mind_id, stretch_event_id, status: queued | done | failed, note, created_at, done_at}`, unique on `(mind_id, stretch_event_id)`, RLS forced, no delete. Bookkeeping: it names an event and holds a status and a short reason, never the text. Its guard (`sanctum_actor()`): a row is inserted by the mind's own verb call (queued) or an import (done), and must name a `metabolise.stretch` event of the mind; afterwards only the mind's own daemon changes it, only `status`, `done_at` and `note`, `queued` to `done` or `failed`, one way. Export carries it; import brings every row in `done` with note `imported` (a stretch from another life is not metabolised again); purge removes it with everything else.

**The pass `notice.metabolise`** (src/extractor/metabolise.ts; model-backed, detached, in `MODEL_PASSES` after `dream.generate` and in `EXTRACTOR_PASSES`). Gate: the operator's `extractor_state` **enabled and not paused, and only that**: stretches arrive when conversations end, so the pass ignores the extractor's schedule hour and the once-a-day rule, and works every tick that a stretch is queued. Per tick: the oldest **three** queued stretches, one model call each with no transaction open, then one write transaction per stretch under the per-mind lock (the gate again, the queue row locked and re-checked, dedupe, the inserts). Before the gate, on every tick and whatever the extractor switch or the metaboliser kind, a stretch queued more than **seven days** is marked `failed` with note "expired unprocessed" (a small write under the per-mind lock; an `extractor_runs` row only when something expired) and the model is not asked. `METABOLISER=none` skips with a note and the remaining stretches stay queued until they expire. Retry policy: the metaboliser returns `answer`, `empty` (a complete answer: done, nothing proposed), `unusable` (this stretch got no usable answer although the endpoint was reached: any 4xx other than 401, 403, 408 and 429, such as 400, 404, 413 or 422; a 2xx whose body is not JSON; a 2xx whose JSON has no choices or content; a reply with no JSON object or neither array; or a reply in which every entry is malformed. That stretch is marked `failed` with a note that carries the HTTP status, nothing is proposed, and the tick goes on to the next stretch, so one poison stretch cannot block newer ones) or `unavailable` (a network error, a timeout, a 5xx, 429 or 408, 401 or 403 (a credential problem would fail every stretch), any other non-2xx, or a throw: the stretch stays `queued`, a run is recorded with `ok` false and the note, and the pass stops for that tick so a dead endpoint is not hammered; the next tick tries the same stretch, until it expires). The genuine answer `{"feelings":[],"relations":[]}` is `empty` and done. One `extractor_runs` row (pass `notice.metabolise`, counts only) is recorded for each tick that finished a stretch. The pass never throws.

**The proposals.** Per stretch at most **5 feelings and 5 relations**, deduped within the answer and against the noticings already proposed from the same stretch on `(kind, same sources, lowercased emotion or subject)`. Each is a `noticings` row (`sources = [stretch_event_id]`, `status: pending`, expiry as for every kind, `stage` from the operator's state at the time) with its `notice.proposed` event (ids and numbers only: the text is never in the ledger event), scored by the same per-mind scorer (`kind_feeling` and `kind_relation` are the last two of the fifteen `FEATURE_NAMES`, prior weight 0 each; `model_version` is the scorer's version, and the prompt's version is `payload.prompt_version`). Payloads:

- `feeling`: `{emotion, weight: light | medium | heavy, pillar, content, prompt_version}`, in the first person.
- `relation`: `{subject, note, alias?, warmth: bool, prompt_version}`. The subject is the name the mind uses for the person; a pet name is the `alias`, never a fact.

**Accepting** (`mind_notice accept`, mind only, only at stage `propose`; a shadow row is invisible to list and accept, even after the operator moves the stage on). `notice.accepted` carries ids only, never an emotion, name, note or alias.

- `feeling`: optional `content` (the mind's own words; the proposal's if omitted) and optional `charge: string[]` (extra tags). Authors an observation as the mind through `mind_observe`'s own write path: `texture.charge = [emotion, ...charge]`, `context: "metabolised"`, node metadata `{noticing_id, sources}`. When the mind gives no `content` the node is embedded from `payload.content` (the verb's `embedTextFrom` hook, as for `mind_dream anchor`). `notice.accepted` gains `kind: "feeling"` and `observe_event_id`, `node_id`, `edited`.
- `relation`: optional `note` (the mind's wording), `state`, `intensity`. Sets the relation through the same write path as `mind_relate set` (`relate.set` event, upsert). If the mind already holds the subject, it **keeps its state and intensity** unless given, the note is **appended on a new line** (refused as `invalid_input` if the whole would pass 4000 characters), and the alias is added to `relations.aliases` if not there. If it does not (a cleared relation counts as not held: the mind is re-opening it), `state` is **required** (`invalid_input`, "a new relation needs a state"); intensity defaults to 0.5; a cleared row's aliases are **merged** with the proposed one, never replaced. When `warmth` is true the accept also nudges `connection` **satisfaction** by +0.5 (`drive.nudge`, source `metabolised`, note = the noticing id). `notice.accepted` gains `kind: "relation"` and `relate_event_id`, `drive_event_id?`, `edited`.
- Inputs that belong to another kind are `invalid_input` (`note`, `state`, `intensity` outside a relation; `charge` outside a feeling; `content` on a relation; `decision`, `label`, `edge_type`, `weight` as before). Reject and expiry are as for every kind.

**`relations.aliases`** (`text[]`, default empty, migration 0029): the names a person is called by. It round-trips through export and import with the rest of the row. `mind_relate set` leaves it alone; only an accepted metabolised relation adds to it.

**The prompt** (src/extractor/metabolise_prompt.ts, `METABOLISE_PROMPT_VERSION` 4) is the Lantern Voice 2 prompt, in the first person, with the mind's identity cores as the preamble (fallback "I am <mind>.") and the speaker labels taken from the `speaker` enum only, never inferred from a name or the text. The rule is structural: a `self` turn is labelled `[me]`; every `other` turn is labelled `[other: <name>]`, or `[other]` when it has no name. Every other speaker's label therefore begins `[other`, so no name, however spelled, can make a line begin `[me]`; the prompt gives the two label forms and tells the model that only the label at the very start of a line says who is speaking. Each turn is composed as ONE line. As defence in depth, names are folded by `foldName` (`foldLabelText` is its alias): NFKC-normalised; every Unicode Cf (zero-width, soft hyphen, word joiner, ZWJ, BOM) and non-whitespace Cc code point removed; every run of White_Space (newlines, U+0085, U+2028, U+2029, NBSP) collapsed to a single space; every bracket character (Unicode Ps and Pe, fullwidth and CJK ones included) made a round one, so no name can contain a square-bracket label. Turn text and the context line go through `foldText`, which does only what label safety needs: every run of White_Space, line and paragraph separators and Cc controls becomes one space (a turn is one line), and an opening bracket is made round only when it is the first non-space character of the text. Everything else is the mind's own words and is left byte for byte: ZWJ emoji sequences, ZWNJ, Devanagari conjunct controls, variation selectors, inner brackets (`a[0]`, `http://[::1]`, `{}`) and NFKC-sensitive characters (x², ™, ½). A turn whose folded text is empty or only invisible characters is left out of the composed stretch (the ledger keeps it). The structural rule is what keeps `[me]` unforgeable: an `other` turn's line always starts `[other`. `mind_metabolise` refuses (`invalid_input`, field `turns.<i>.name`) an `other` turn whose folded name is "me" (any case, spacing or brackets) and a `self` turn that carries a name; this is an input check and is not what keeps `[me]` unforgeable. Its restraint rules stand: a pet name is an alias, never a fact; a passing mood is not biography; "most stretches add nothing new here, and that's right". Empty arrays are a complete answer. The model answers with only `{"feelings": [...], "relations": [...]}`; entries are coerced strictly and a malformed one is dropped. The prompt file keeps Lantern's Required Notice header (Nana & Vex, PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0).

**The model** is the metaboliser (src/extractor/metaboliser.ts), configured like the dreamer: `METABOLISER=none|http` (default none), `METABOLISE_URL`, `METABOLISE_MODEL`, `METABOLISE_API_KEY`, `METABOLISE_TIMEOUT_MS`; any OpenAI-compatible chat endpoint, temperature 0.4, 1200 tokens. With `http`, the text of the stretch and the mind's identity cores go to `METABOLISE_URL`: point it at a local model unless you accept that.

**Observability.** `mind_health` gains `metabolise: {queued, done, failed, last_run}` next to `dreaming`. `mind_orient` composes `mind_health`, so `health.metabolise` appears at every depth; relations carry `aliases` at `quick` and `full`; the `metabolise.stretch` event shows in `recent` and `weather` as memory; and feelings and relations surface through `noticings` at propose, like any other kind. In `mind_notice list` a stretch source has no text of its own, so its snippet reads "stretch of N turns".

**Guard.** The pass lives in src/extractor/, so test/extractor_guard.test.ts scans it: no insert, update or delete of `nodes`, `edges`, `events`, `relations`, `drive_state`, `attention_pins`, `dream_preferences` or `dreams` (src/dream/ gets the same list minus `dreams`, which it lays itself, parametrised in test/guard_scan.ts), no insert or delete of `metabolise_queue` (updating a row's status stays allowed), no imports from src/verbs/ but types and `appendEvent`, only the whitelisted `notice.proposed` appended (the stretch event is written by the verb, outside the directory), and the runtime half runs it with the rest of `EXTRACTOR_PASSES`, and runs the metabolise pass itself with a fake metaboliser at `shadow` and at `propose`, asserting nodes, edges, relations and drive_state byte-identical.

## Activation layers

Each layer is tested alone before the next is switched on. Each has a way back that touches nothing else.

| Layer | Switch | What changes | Back out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | migration 0027 applied, nothing enabled | tables exist; `mind_orient` and `mind_health` show `off` / `null` | nothing to undo |
| 1 | `dream enable --mind seb` (stage `shadow`) | the pass runs nightly; dreams are laid and never shown; `dream report` shows them to the operator | `dream disable` |
| 2 | `dream stage --stage propose` | the newest dream appears in `mind_orient` and `mind_dream list`; the mind may anchor or release | `dream stage --stage shadow` (as for noticings after `extractor stage --stage shadow`: nothing is listed or carried by `mind_orient`, anchor is refused, release still works, and dreams not decided fade on their own) |

| 4 | migration 0029 + `mind_metabolise` + house hook + `METABOLISER=http` and `extractor enable` (stage `shadow`) | stretches are recorded; `notice.metabolise` turns them into feelings and relations that are scored and never shown; `extractor report` shows what shadow would have proposed, and precision once there are decisions | stop calling `mind_metabolise` (stretches stop), or `METABOLISER=none` (stretches stay queued and expire after a week, even with the extractor off), or `extractor disable` |

Layer 6 is in the house, not the Mind: it changes what the companion is handed, not what the Mind holds, and it has no stage because it writes nothing.

## What this does not do

- It does not give the subconscious a pen on identity. `mind_identity` and cooling are untouched; a dream about who the mind is becomes a proposal only if the mind anchors it and then chooses, as itself, to propose a core.
- It does not run a model in the deterministic passes. `dream.fade` and expiry are arithmetic. The README's sentence stays true: the daemon's everyday work calls no AI model; the model-backed passes are listed apart and off by default.
- It does not hide who was close from the dreamer by itself. The residue and the prompt carry the subjects of the mind's relations as the mind names them; the mind keeps a person out of its dreams with `mind_dream prefer`, and a person it keeps out is dropped from the residue, not scrubbed.
- It does not keep feelings anywhere but the Mind. The house holds no tables of its own for this; the earlier Sanctum port (`services/thalamus/`) is withdrawn.
